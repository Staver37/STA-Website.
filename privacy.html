Privacy Policy

How information submitted through our website is handled.

LAST UPDATEDNovember 23, 2026

For privacy-related requests, contact office@stafreight.com.

This policy explains how STA Freight Solution LLC (branded online as STA Freight Solutions) handles information that visitors submit through the website at stafreight.com.

Information submitted

Depending on the form used, you may provide your name, company name, email address, phone number, pickup and delivery locations, equipment type, requested shipping date and shipment details.

Why we collect information

We use submitted information to review freight inquiries, communicate about load requirements and respond to quote requests. Please do not submit passwords, payment card numbers, Social Security numbers or sensitive personal data in free-text fields.

Form processing

Our quote forms use FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service, to process submissions and forward them to our business email. That provider may process submission information and technical data according to its own terms and privacy practices. A spam-prevention check may appear when a form is submitted.

Other website services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its hosting provider may process technical data such as IP address and browser information when the site is accessed. Refer to the provider's published privacy policy for details.

Questions, corrections and deletion requests

For questions about submitted information, including access, correction or deletion requests, email office@stafreight.com. Requests are reviewed in accordance with applicable requirements and business recordkeeping needs.

Changes to this policy

We may update this notice if our website or data-handling practices change. The revision date at the top identifies the current version.
