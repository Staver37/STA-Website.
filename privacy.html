Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This page explains how STA Freight Solution LLC (doing business online as STA Freight Solutions) handles the information visitors submit using the quote request forms on stafreight.com.

Information submitted

Depending on which form you use, we receive information you provide, which may include your name, company name, email address, phone number, pickup and delivery locations, equipment type, requested shipping date, and shipment details.

Why this information is collected

Submitted information is used to review freight inquiries, communicate about shipment requirements, and respond to quote requests. Please do not include passwords, payment card numbers, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive personal information in the free-text fields.

Form processing

Quote forms use FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, to process submissions and forward the details to our business email. FormSubmit may process submitted details and related technical information according to its own terms and privacy practices. A spam-prevention check may appear when you submit a form.

Other services

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages and loads an image from Unsplash. Those providers may process technical data, such as IP address and browser information, when their resources are requested. You can consult their published privacy policies for details.

Questions or requests about your information

For questions about information submitted through this website, including requests about access, correction or deletion, contact office@stafreight.com. Requests will be reviewed in light of applicable requirements and business recordkeeping needs.

Changes to this notice

This policy may be updated if the website or its data-handling practices change. The date at the top will indicate the latest revision.
