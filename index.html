FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

The details, answered.

Before you send a lane, here are a few useful things to know.

What details do I need for a freight quote?

Pickup and delivery locations, requested dates, equipment type, commodity, weight and any special instructions. Dimensions, appointments and number of stops may also be relevant.

Does the planning estimator provide a guaranteed rate?

No. It provides only an illustrative range. A final quote depends on the full lane and load details and current carrier availability.

What equipment can STA Freight coordinate?

You can inquire about dry van, refrigerated, flatbed, step deck and specialized freight. Availability depends on verified carrier capacity and shipment requirements.

How are carriers reviewed?

Authority, insurance and relevant carrier documentation are part of the review process before a shipment is assigned.

Can I request a quote online?

Yes. Use the Request a Quote form or Quick Quote button. Your submission is sent securely for review by our team.
