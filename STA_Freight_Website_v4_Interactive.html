FAQ

Answers before the first call.

A concise FAQ helps customers understand the process and reduces unnecessary back-and-forth.

What information do you need for an exact freight quote?

Pickup and delivery locations, requested dates, equipment type, commodity, weight, dimensions when relevant, number of stops, and any appointment or special handling requirements.

Does the online estimator show a guaranteed rate?

No. It is a broad planning tool only. Final freight pricing depends on the actual lane, date, capacity, commodity, weight, appointments, and market conditions.

What equipment types can STA Freight support?

The site is structured around dry van, refrigerated, flatbed, step deck, and specialized freight needs, subject to verified carrier availability.

How are carriers verified?

Before dispatch, carrier authority, insurance, identity/contact details, and required documentation should be checked as part of the onboarding and load-booking process.

How will shipment updates work?

The goal is proactive communication through booking, pickup, transit, exceptions, delivery, and final documentation so customers do not have to chase basic status information.

Can I request a quote without calling?

Yes. Use the short quote form or the Quick Quote button. The form opens an email with the shipment details already organized for STA Freight.
